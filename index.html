About Lesotho

Lesotho is a small country that is completely surrounded by South Africa. It is called the Kingdom in the Sky because the whole country is more than 1,000 metres above sea level.

The highest point is Thabana Ntlenyana at 3,482 metres. Visitors come to Lesotho to hike, ride ponies, see waterfalls and enjoy the Basotho culture.

This website will help you plan your trip.

Quick Facts

  • Capital city: Maseru
  • Languages: Sesotho and English
  • Currency: Loti (M)
  • Highest point: 3,482 m

Travel Tips

Pack warm clothes, a jacket, walking shoes and sunscreen. The weather in the mountains can change very quickly.

Summer (October to March) is warm and green. Winter (June to August) is cold and it can snow in the mountains.
